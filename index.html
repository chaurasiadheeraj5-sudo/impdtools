impdtools - Quick Guide & Tips

📐 DPI & Print Quality

DPI (Dots Per Inch) controls the resolution when printing. 300 DPI is standard photographic print quality. Our tools write true JFIF 300 DPI headers into exported JPEG files so printers and photo studios recognize the exact physical scale.

📷 Standard Passport & Visa Sizes

  • India / UK / Schengen / Australia Passport: 3.5 × 4.5 cm (35 × 45 mm)
  • US Visa / Passport / OCI: 2 × 2 inches (5.08 × 5.08 cm / 51 × 51 mm)
  • Standard Stamp Size: 2.0 × 2.5 cm (20 × 25 mm)
  • Japan / Asian ID: 3.0 × 4.0 cm (30 × 40 mm)

🖨️ Printing Instructions (100% Scale)

When printing your generated PDF or sheet from Chrome, Edge, or Adobe Acrobat:

  1. Choose paper size matching your sheet (e.g. A4 or 4×6").
  2. Set Scale to 100% or "Actual Size" (Do NOT choose "Fit to Printable Area").
  3. Turn on Border and Cut marks in our tool for effortless scissor trimming.

🔒 100% Privacy & Offline Support

All image and PDF processing is performed entirely in your browser using Web APIs and WebAssembly. No data is sent to any external server.
